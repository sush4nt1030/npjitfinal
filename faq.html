About NISC

What is NISC?

NISC, the Nepalgunj IT Students Community, is a free community for students in Nepalgunj who want to learn technology, connect with others, build projects and grow. Read more.

Where is NISC based?

Nepalgunj, in Banke district, Lumbini Province, Nepal. The venue for each activity is listed on its event page.

What has NISC done so far?

Since starting on 18 November 2023, NISC has grown from an online group of around 291 members to a community with its own core team, and has successfully completed two sessions (the first on 31 August 2025). You can explore them in the event archive and see every milestone on Our Journey.

Who runs NISC?

NISC was founded by Sushant Budha and Bharat Rawal, who run it as Admins. They are joined by Dinesh Sharma (Tech Lead), Shristy Soni (Community Lead), Sushil Rawot (Content / Design Lead), Nischal Koirala (Operations / Events Lead) and Bishal Banjara (Content Lead / Editor). Meet the team.

Joining

Is NISC free to join?

Yes. Joining NISC is free.

Who can join?

+2 students, Bachelor students, IT students, non-IT students interested in technology, beginners, learners, designers, developers, creators, entrepreneurs and technology enthusiasts. See who NISC is for.

I’m not an IT student. Can I still join?

Yes. If technology interests you, you’re welcome, whatever you study.

I’m a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. You don’t need to be an expert. You just need the willingness to learn, contribute, and grow.

How do I join?

Fill in the Join form or message NISC on Facebook or Instagram (Nepalgunj IT student’s community). The team then adds you to the community groups, where sessions and events are announced.

Events & registration

How do I register for an event?

Open the event from the Events page and choose Register now. The form asks for your contact details, studies, and what you hope to learn.

Why do you ask for my WhatsApp number?

WhatsApp is the main channel for reminders, venue details and last-minute changes, so participants don’t miss anything.

Do events cost anything?

Joining NISC is free. Each event page lists the details for that event. If you’re unsure, ask the team before registering.

Will I get a certificate?

Not every NISC activity comes with a certificate. If an event offers one, it will be mentioned in the event details or its announcement.

Is NISC part of WordCamp Nepal 2027?

WordCamp Nepal 2027 is organised by the WordPress community in Nepal and comes to Nepalgunj on 29–30 January 2027 at Siddharth Cottage Restaurant. NISC is supporting it locally and invites every member to take part. See the event page or nepal.wordcamp.org/2027.

Can I suggest a topic or speak at an event?

Yes, please do. Suggest an event.

Projects & opportunities

How can my project be featured?

Send the details through the contact form. The project must be real and you must be one of its contributors. The team checks the details with you before publishing.

How are opportunities verified?

A team member confirms each opportunity on the organiser’s official page, and the listing shows who checked it and when. Always read the official details before applying, and never pay anyone to apply.

Can I share an opportunity?

Yes. Send it to the team with the official link.

Website & privacy

What happens to the information I submit?

It’s used only for the purpose of the form, such as organising an event or welcoming you to the community. Read the Privacy Policy.

How is information on this website checked?

NISC only publishes verified information. Anything still being confirmed is clearly marked, and numbers appear only when they come from real records.
