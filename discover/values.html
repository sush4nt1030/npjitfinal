Our values

Six principles that shape how NISC members learn, work and treat each other.

Curiosity first

Every question is a good question. We learn by asking, exploring and trying things.

In practiceBeginners are encouraged to ask during sessions and discussions.

Everyone is welcome

IT or non-IT, beginner or experienced, from any college. NISC is open to every student who wants to learn.

In practiceFree to join, and friendly to first-timers.

Learn by building

Knowledge becomes skill when you use it. We turn what we learn into projects.

In practiceMembers build together and share their work in the showcase.

Share what you know

The best way to learn is to teach. Members help each other grow.

In practicePeer learning, study circles and member-led sessions.

Respect and integrity

We treat each other with respect, give credit where it’s due, and act honestly, including publishing only verified information.

In practiceThe Code of Conduct applies in every NISC space.

Grow together

Individual success means more when it lifts others too.

In practiceOpportunities are shared openly with the whole community.
