Mission & vision

What NISC does every day, and the future it’s working towards.

Our mission

To help students in Nepalgunj learn technology together, connect with each other, build real projects, and grow into confident professionals and creators.

Our vision

A Nepalgunj where every student who is curious about technology has a community to learn with, build with, and grow with.

What we’re working towards

Four goals turn the mission into everyday work.

Accessible learning

Learning sessions and workshops that welcome every level, from first-timers to experienced students.

Real experience

Projects, competitions and collaboration that give students work they can show.

Connected students

Friendships and teams that reach across colleges, faculties and skill sets.

A lasting archive

Every activity documented, so knowledge and history stay with the community year after year.
