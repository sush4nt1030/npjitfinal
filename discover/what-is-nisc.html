What is NISC?

A plain-language introduction to the Nepalgunj IT Students Community.

NISC in one sentence

NISC is a free community where students in Nepalgunj learn technology together, connect with each other, build real projects, and grow towards their goals.

The name

NISC stands for Nepalgunj IT Students Community. Each word tells you something. Nepalgunj is where we are. IT is what brings us together. Students are who we are for. And community is how we work: together, not alone.

What happens at NISC

NISC organises activities that help students move from curiosity to real skill:

  • Learning sessions and workshops on technology topics, for different levels.
  • Meetups and discussions where students share what they’re learning.
  • Project building, so knowledge turns into something you can show.
  • Opportunities such as internships, hackathons and scholarships, shared after they’re verified.
  • Volunteering, for students who want to help run the community.

NISC is

  • A learning community for students
  • Free to join
  • Open to beginners and non-IT students
  • A place to build real projects with others
  • A public archive of everything the community does

NISC is not

  • A paid course or coaching centre
  • Only for experts or top students
  • Only for IT students
  • A place to just watch. You’re invited to take part

Where NISC stands today

NISC has successfully completed 2 sessions and is growing its team, its activities and now its digital home. You can follow every step on Our Journey.
