Who NISC is for

If you’re a student in or around Nepalgunj and you’re curious about technology, NISC is for you.

You don’t need to be an expert. You just need the willingness to learn, contribute, and grow.

+2 students

Explore technology early, find out what you enjoy, and meet seniors who can guide your next step.

Bachelor students

Go beyond the syllabus with practical sessions, projects and opportunities.

IT students

Whether you study BSc CSIT, BCA, BIT, BIM, computer engineering or another IT programme, find people to build with.

Non-IT students

Studying management, humanities, science or anything else? If technology interests you, you belong here too.

Beginners

Never written a line of code? Perfect. Every expert started exactly where you are.

Self-taught learners

Learning from videos and courses? Get feedback, accountability and study partners.

Designers

UI/UX, graphics and branding skills are needed in every project team.

Developers

Share what you know, review code, and build bigger things with a team.

Creators

Content, video, writing and photography help the community tell its story.

Entrepreneurs

Find technical co-builders and test ideas with people who can make them real.

Technology enthusiasts

Love talking about tech, trying new tools or following what’s next? Join the conversation.

Future volunteers

Want to organise, lead or help others? There’s a role for you in running NISC.

Not sure if you fit?

You probably do. Read the FAQ or ask the team.
