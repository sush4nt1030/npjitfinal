How to join

Four simple steps

  1. Say hello

    Tell us a little about yourself and what you’re interested in, or message NISC on Facebook or Instagram: Nepalgunj IT student’s community.

  2. Join the WhatsApp community

  3. Come to an event

    Your first session is the best way to meet everyone. See events.

  4. Start contributing

    Ask, share, build and help. There’s no minimum.

Ways to contribute

Help run the community

NISC grows because members step up. Pick something you enjoy, or something you want to learn.

Mention your interest in the form below, or tell the team directly.

  • Event support: planning, venues, registration desks and logistics.
  • Content & design: posters, social posts, writing and event recaps.
  • Tech & website: this website, tools and project mentoring.
  • Outreach: welcoming new students and reaching other colleges.
  • Speaking: sharing what you know in a session or workshop.
  • Documentation: photos and notes that keep the archive alive.
