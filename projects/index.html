How to add your project

  1. Build it

    Alone or with other NISC members. Any size counts, from a small tool to a full app.

  2. Document it

    Write a short README, take a few screenshots, and note what you learned.

  3. Send it

    Share the details through the contact form. The team checks it with you.

  4. Get featured

    Your project gets its own page here, linked to your profile and the events it came from.
